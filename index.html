The AI, privacy and fairness

What the AI does — and doesn't

Plain code does all arithmetic, searches and feasibility checks. An optional small language model (Qwen2.5 0.5B, running in your browser) only rephrases the checked answer. It can be wrong, doesn't see your bank, and doesn't know local costs. Without it, the coach still answers using the same calculations.

Privacy & fairness

No bank credentials or personal details are requested. Guest budgets stay in this browser. With a cloud account, each budget row is protected so only its owner can read it. Everyone gets the same guidance. A 20% savings rate is an example benchmark, not a requirement; high living costs, dependants, debt and irregular income can make it unrealistic. Debt isn't ranked because interest rates aren't collected.

Budget guide

A budget is a snapshot of money in and money out. "Unplanned" money is income minus what you entered. It isn't automatically spare: annual bills, medical costs or family support may need it. Some courses use a 50/30/20 split for needs, wants and savings. Treat it as a prompt, not a prescription.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this without an account?

Yes. Choose "Try as guest". Your numbers are saved only in this browser.

Does the AI see my bank account?

No. There is no bank connection. It only sees what you type.

Why does the AI need a download?

The model runs on your device instead of a remote service. It is about 500 MB, downloads once only if you choose to enable it, and needs a recent Chrome or Edge with WebGPU.

Can this replace a financial advisor?

No. It's an educational prototype, not financial, tax, credit or investment advice.
